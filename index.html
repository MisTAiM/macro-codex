Wave Management Math

The most controllable macro lever in the game. Master waves and tempo follows automatically.

Waves are the game's clock. Every wave is ~150-220 gold and ~500 XP. Missing 3 waves early is roughly half a level deficit. Your wave state dictates whether you have tempo — and tempo dictates whether you win objective fights.
The Four Wave States
Freeze
Maintain 3-5 more enemy minions alive than yours, near your tower. Enemy must walk into danger to farm. You are safe. They bleed XP and gold.
HOW: Kill only what keeps the ratio. Let their wave push, kill just enough of yours. Never kill more than needed. If it breaks, slow push to reset.

WHEN: You won lane, enemy is weaker, jungler is coming to dive, or you're protecting a lead.
Slow Push
Kill the enemy backline (casters) first. Let your melee minions stack. Wave grows large over time without you needing to auto anything extra.
HOW: Kill casters early in each wave cycle. Last-hit only. Your wave inflates automatically.

WHEN: Before an objective (crash that big wave to it), before a roam, or before a dive setup. The big wave creates the opportunity.
Crash
Full clear and push everything into the enemy tower. Tower spends time killing minions — enemy loses those CS if they aren't there. You gain free tempo.
HOW: Hard clear everything. Run it into the tower. Leave as it crashes.

WHEN: 20-30s before an objective, before recalling, before a roam, after killing enemy, to deny TP value. Every action you take after a crash costs them something.
Bounce
After a crash, the enemy wave rebuilds and naturally pushes toward you. The wave "bounces" back. You return to lane into a safe setup for a freeze.
HOW: After crashing, go do your thing (recall, objective). Return when the bounced wave reaches you.

WHEN: Automatically happens after any crash. Your job is to USE the bounce window (recall, roam) and return in time to collect.
Wave Gold & XP Math
Normal Wave Gold Value 6 melee @ ~21g each = 126g
3 casters @ ~17g each = 51g
Total: ~177g per normal wave
Cannon wave every 3rd: +~60g = ~237g
XP Per Wave (early, approx) Full wave: ~500 XP (scales with game time)
Missing 3 waves = ~1,500 XP lost
Level 4–6: 3 missed waves ≈ half a level behind
This is why crashes + bounces compound advantage so fast
Crash Timing Formula
The Crash-Before-Objective Rule Objective spawns: T
Start crashing wave by: T − 40 seconds
Arrive at objective: T − 10 to 15 seconds
If you're still farming at T-20, you are ALREADY LATE. Priority shift: leave wave and run.
Never finish the last three minions if it means arriving at objective after it spawns.
Freeze Formula (Exact) Minions on your side > Minions on enemy side
Target: enemy has 3–5 more minions than yours
This keeps the wave stationary near your tower. If it drifts toward enemy tower, you over-killed.
Cannon waves make freezes harder — kill cannon early or the wave explodes in size.
When to Roam — All Conditions Must Be True
  • ✓
    Your wave is crashing OR already crashed into the enemy tower
    If your wave is pushing TO you → stay and collect. You lose 150-220g and 500 XP per wave if you leave now.
  • ✓
    You have lane priority (enemy is pushed in or recalled)
    No priority = you arrive to the fight late and your presence doesn't swing it. You just wasted a wave for nothing.
  • ✓
    There is an active objective or confirmed kill opportunity to convert
    Roaming "to see what's happening" is not a plan. Have a destination and a purpose before you leave lane.
  • ✓
    You are healthy enough to fight when you arrive
    60% HP roam to "help" means you feed two kills instead of one. Recall first if needed — the wave will still be there on your return.
  • ✓
    You have the mobility to get there in time to matter
    Count walk time. Bot lane from top lane in 2026 takes ~18-22 seconds. If the fight is already decided by then, TP or don't go.
The #1 Wave Error in Gold Elo: Letting your wave randomly crash into the enemy tower with no plan attached to it. You hand them free gold AND gave yourself zero tempo. Every crash must be deliberate — tied to a recall, roam, dive, or objective. If you can't answer "what am I doing with this crash?", you don't have a game plan.
Advanced: Side Lane Wave Management (Mid-Late Game)
Shoving Side Lanes Before Baron (The Setup) At 19:00 → push your assigned side lane hard into the enemy tower
This forces: enemy must respond to the wave (they lose tower) OR they don't (you gain pressure)
Then rotate to Baron at 19:30–19:45
You now have: Baron threat + a side wave threatening a tower = two problems they must solve simultaneously

2026 Kill Gold Devaluation System — Know This
This was doubled in Patch 26.1. The devaluation rate went from 0.2 to 0.4, and bounty accrual doubled from 0.1 to 0.2. Dying is now twice as punishing to your own kill gold — and your fed carry becomes a target twice as fast.

Example: You die, giving the enemy 300g
Your next kill is worth: 300 − (300 × 0.4) = −120g vs old −60g

Your fed carry is twice the target they used to be. Play around them smarter — or let them split where it's safe.

Item Spike Timing — The Mental Model
Force Fights When
  • ↑
    You just completed your first item, enemy hasn't
    Biggest power delta in the game. This window closes in minutes. Force it NOW.
  • ↑
    You hit your legendary / mythic upgrade before them
    Item spikes are time-gated. Your window is 1-3 minutes wide before they catch up.
  • ↑
    Your comp naturally spikes earlier than theirs
    Engage comp at 20 min vs scale comp at 35 min = fight at 18 min and win before they power up.
Stall / Avoid Fights When
  • ↓
    Enemy just completed a major item and you haven't
    You are in the deficit window. Farm waves, don't fight. Let the window close.
  • ↓
    Your comp is designed to win 30-35 minutes in
    Your job is to survive until your power window. Don't let them force you into fights at minute 15.
  • ↓
    You're 1-2 items behind the fed enemy carry
    Item deficit = fight deficit. Peel for your own fed player instead.
The Recall Discipline Formula
Optimal Recall Conditions (All Should Be True) ✓ Your wave is crashing / crashed (you lose 0 CS)
✓ You have 1,400-1,600g+ (component or full item on recall)
✓ No objective spawning in the next 90 seconds
✓ Your lane opponent has similar HP / is also recalling

A well-timed recall = full item advantage + safe return into a bounced wave.
Bad recall timing = losing 1-2 waves of gold (300-440g) + arriving back to a pushed wave you can't freeze.

Mid-Game Systems

When laning ends, most players default to following their teammates. Don't. Have a system.

The Single Most Important Macro Skill: Knowing what to do in the 5 seconds after a kill or fight ends. That 5-second window determines whether you convert wins into leads or let them evaporate. Run the priority system below — every single time.
Post-Fight Priority — Run This In Order, Every Time
1
Can we end the game RIGHT NOW?
If yes: ignore everything else. Walk to their Nexus. Don't round up. Don't recall. Don't "play it safe." You will never get a better window. GO.
2
Is Baron spawning / available in the next 90 seconds?
If yes: rotate immediately. Vision first, then position. Smite ready. Baron at 20:00 — if you won a fight at 19:30, that Baron is yours. Claim it.
3
Is Dragon spawning / available in the next 90 seconds?
If yes: rotate to Dragon. Every stack compounds. Trading towers for a Dragon stack is usually worth it early (it's not worth it if you're 3-0 on stacks and need an inhib).
4
Is there a tower in our path we can take with the numbers advantage?
If yes: push it down. Towers give 150-300g split + map access. Push in the direction of the fight — never regroup across the map just to group up again.
5
Should we reset for items?
Only if: no objectives available in 90s, your gold puts you within 300g of a major item spike, AND your side waves are in a safe state. Otherwise you're wasting tempo.
6
Push side lanes and set up the next play cycle.
Crash a side wave. Ward the next objective area. Force the enemy to make a decision. Repeat from Step 1 next fight.
The 60-30-10 Objective Setup Rule
T−60 Seconds Vision Phase

Ward the river entrances, the pit itself, and the flanking bushes. Sweep enemy wards from your side. Your whole team should be on the correct side of the map. Not farming. Not in base. Moving.

T−30 Seconds Stack Phase

Stop farming. Everyone is at the objective. No stragglers collecting "one more wave." Check the minimap for enemy positions. If you don't know where 3+ enemies are, do not start the objective.

T−10 Seconds Execute Phase

Smite is ready. Frontline is tanking. Formation is set. Engage on THEM if they approach — don't let them contest on their terms. You arrived first. You dictate.

Win Condition Framework — Answer Before Every Game
Ask At 2:00, 8:00, and 15:00
→Who is the strongest player on our team right now? Build the game around them. Protect them. Feed them objectives.
→Who is the strongest player on their team right now? Deny them farm, deny them objectives, kill them before Baron fights.
→What is our win condition? Scale, pick, split, engage, or siege. Say it out loud. All decisions flow from this.
→When does our win condition peak vs theirs? Force fights on YOUR timeline, not theirs.
Map Patterns — How to Position 5 People
1-3-1 Split
One strong champion splits top, one splits bot, three control mid and vision. The side laners must draw enemy attention or the pattern fails. Group only for Baron, Elder, or a winning teamfight.
Use when: Your comp has two champions who can 1v1/1v2 in side lanes and disengage safely.
Math: You drawing 2 enemies + your 4 take Baron = net win. Drawing 0 = you're useless. Group.
4-1 Death Push
One champion splits a side lane drawing a response, four group mid to siege, take objectives, or wait for a pick. The 1 must create a 2v1 or threaten a tower or this pattern does nothing.
Use when: You have a fed tank, bruiser, or hyperscaler who can splitpush and teleport back to fights.
Math: 1 drawing 2 = 4v3 mid. Even draw = neutral. 1 drawing 0 = group immediately.
5v5 Grouping
Everyone runs mid together and forces a fight, siege, or tower take. Works when your teamfight comp needs numbers to activate, or when you're ahead and want to close the game fast.
Use when: You have engage/AoE teamfight, you're decisively ahead, or it's late enough that individual picks don't matter.
Math: Every second you're grouped and not taking a tower or objective is gold-per-second wasted.
Pick / Flank
Deep vision, bait with one player, collapse on the isolated enemy. Works with assassins, engage supports, or any pick-focused comp. Requires excellent vision control around the bait point.
Use when: You have hard CC + mobility, and the enemy team is soft when isolated.
Math: 1 pick → 5v4 for 25-40s at level 13 → guaranteed objective if you move fast.
Role Quests 2026 — Track These
New in Season 2026: Role Quests were added to every position (not just support/jungle). Each role gets a unique power reward when their lane-phase quest completes. Know when YOUR quest completes. Know when your opponent's quest completes. Force fights after yours, before theirs.

The Visibility Rule: If you cannot see 3 or more enemy champions on the minimap, do not walk forward into fog. This is not optional. Every death to an ambush is your fault — not bad luck.
Ward vs. Sweep — Decision Tree
Decision
Is an objective spawning in the next 90 seconds?
Yes →
Sweep first, then plant. Clear enemy wards in the pit and river entrances BEFORE placing yours. A ward you plant while their ward sees you = they know your position and can counter-engage. Order: Sweep pit → Sweep river → Plant deep offensive wards.
No →
Defensive plant. River bushes, tribrush on your side, and dragon/baron side approach paths. Save your sweeper for pre-objective timing — don't waste Oracle charges on random roaming lanes.
Decision
Did you just win a fight and have 20-40 seconds of dead enemies?
Yes →
Deep ward immediately. Place wards in their jungle — blue side, their camps, their lane bushes. This is your free window. Support especially: this is your primary job in this moment. Don't recall yet.
No →
Stay in safe warding positions. Never deep ward when enemies are alive and unaccounted for. You will die and lose both the ward and your own death timer.
Mandatory Wards by Game Phase
Early Game (0:00–14:00)
  • →
    River bush (your side)
    Gank protection. Always. No exceptions. If you push without this, you deserve the gank.
  • →
    Enemy jungle entrance when you're pushing
    Only ever push when this path is warded. Otherwise you're farming into darkness.
  • →
    Dragon / Grub pit at 4:00
    Gives you vision of dragon side approach before 5:00 spawn. Wins information wars.
  • →
    Track enemy jungler after every visible camp clear
    Jungler clears red at 2:00 top side → they're pathing top or mid, not bot. Dragon is free. Ping it.
Mid Game (15:00–25:00)
  • →
    Both Baron river entrances by 18:30
    Baron spawns at 20:00. If you're planting Baron vision at 19:45 you've already lost the vision war.
  • →
    Deep enemy jungle — know where their camps are
    Seeing their jungler doing wraiths at 19:00 tells you exactly where NOT to get caught.
  • →
    Pixel brush / Dragon side tri-brush
    Dragon side vision = safe bot lane poke + flanking protection in teamfights.
  • ✕
    SWEEP enemy wards before starting Baron
    If they have a ward in the pit, they see your HP, they know your smite timing, they counter at the worst moment. Never start Baron with enemy wards alive in the pit.
Faelights — 2026 New Vision Layer
New in Season 2026: Faelights are a new vision system added on top of traditional wards. They provide temporal and positional information in ways that traditional wards didn't. Track enemy Faelight positions — they reveal rotation intentions earlier than ward patterns used to. When you see an enemy Faelight moving toward an objective side, start your own setup immediately.

Why You Take Baron (2026)

Baron is no longer primarily a gold play. The global gold was cut almost in half. Baron is now an XP play. 650 global XP can push your entire team to the next level simultaneously — unlocking item slots, ability rank-ups, and level-scaling abilities all at once.

The minion buff is still the conversion tool — empowered super minions push towers and force the enemy to play defense or watch their base melt.

The Baron Playbook — Four Phases
1
Threat — Create the Dilemma
3 players in Baron pit starting the objective. 2 players split-pushing side lanes hard. The enemy must choose: give up the towers on the sides, or come 5v3 at Baron. Either answer is wrong for them.
Math: If they come 5v3, your 2 split pushers take free towers. If they don't, you get Baron.
2
Bait — Control the Tempo
Start Baron damage to force their hand. If they don't contest, finish it. If they contest, assess: do you have the numbers advantage? Is their position bad? Can your smite win the steal?
Execute conditions: numbers advantage + vision control + waves pushing in + smite available.
3
Execute — Secure It Cleanly
Smite holder is the priority protect target. Everyone else peels for them. Smite Baron at around 1,500-2,000 HP if contested — earlier in the value range if enemy smite is up. Never lose Baron to a smite steal when you had it.
Smite damage: ~900 base at level 18. Time it for when Baron is within 1,000 HP if contested.
4
Convert — Don't Waste the Buff
Baron buff lasts 3 minutes. After securing: push the nearest inhibitor lane with the empowered minions. Do NOT group in fog and look for fights. The buff is the weapon — use it to siege and end, not to brawl.
Bleeding buff time in fog = throwing. Every second of Baron buff not applied to a tower is wasted.
Baron Setup Checklist — Run Before Every Baron Fight
  • ✓
    Vision established — pit, both river entrances, flanking bushes
    If you're missing vision on any entrance, they can engage you from an angle you can't defend. Don't start without full vision.
  • ✓
    Enemy wards in the pit have been swept
    Their ward in the pit tells them your HP at all times. They can smite steal perfectly with that information.
  • ✓
    Side waves are shoved into their towers
    Enemy must lose farm if they contest Baron. This makes contesting costly even if they win the fight.
  • ✓
    We have a numbers advantage (they're 1-2 down)
    5v5 Baron fights go to the team with better comp or better position — extremely risky. Take Baron with numbers whenever possible.
  • ✓
    Smite is available or Baron is at 30%+ HP
    Starting Baron at 30% HP without smite when contested = you're gifting them the steal. Either have smite or start earlier and take it before they arrive.
  • ✕
    Your side waves are NOT pushing toward you
    If waves are crashing into your towers, contesting Baron means losing both Baron AND your towers. Wait 30 seconds and crash the wave first.
The Most Common Baron Throw: Securing Baron then immediately walking into a bad fight because "we have Baron buff." Baron does not win teamfights. Your comp wins teamfights. Baron helps you siege and snowball through minions — not through ego fights in the river.

Game Phase Checklists

Print this section. Read it between games. Internalize it until it's automatic.

PRE-GAME — Before You Lock In
  • 01
    What is OUR win condition?
    Scale and 5v5 / Pick and snowball / Split push / Hard engage / Siege and poke. One answer. Commit to it. Say it in chat.
  • 02
    What is THEIR win condition?
    Scale comp = force early fights. Engage comp = deny vision. Pick comp = move in groups. Identify it and deny it.
  • 03
    When does OUR comp peak? When does THEIRS?
    If you peak at 20 min and they peak at 35 min: force fights at 18 min. Never let them reach their window.
  • 04
    Who is MY matchup and what is the expected outcome?
    Favored = push for kills and priority. Unfavored = farm, scale, and deny them kill pressure on you.
LANING PHASE — Run This Loop Every 2 Minutes
  • →
    What is my current wave state? Is it where I want it?
    Pushed = you have priority and can roam. Frozen = you're denying them. Crashing = you have a tempo window.
  • →
    Where is the enemy jungler?
    If unseen for 90+ seconds and your river is dark → respect the gank. Pull back. Don't be greedy.
  • →
    Is an objective spawning in the next 2 minutes?
    If yes: start your crash now. Arriving with priority 30s before spawn = you control that objective.
  • →
    What are the enemy summoner spell cooldowns?
    Their Flash is down (5 min CD) = you can all-in / dive / they can't escape. Track it on a timer.
  • →
    What is my current HP and should I recall?
    Below 40% = recall if wave is crashed. Don't stay low just to squeeze out 2 more CS. It's a false economy.
  • →
    Am I completing my Role Quest objective?
    Season 2026 — your quest power spike is a real weapon. Track your progress and play toward completing it.
POST-FIGHT — Before You Do Anything Else
  • 1
    How many enemies died? How long are they dead?
    Level 13 = ~40s dead. Level 15 = ~47s. You have a concrete window. Start the clock in your head. Don't waste it.
  • 2
    Can we end right now?
    If yes: go. Skip everything else. The game being over is worth more than every objective combined.
  • 3
    What's the nearest objective we can take?
    Run the priority list: End > Baron > Dragon > Tower. Take the highest-priority thing you can reach.
  • 4
    Is there a tower in our path?
    Always push in the direction of the fight before regrouping. Never regroup across the map empty-handed.
  • 5
    How much gold do I have? Is a major item within 300g?
    If you can recall and return before the next objective window, do it. If not, hold off and convert first.
  • 6
    Are side waves building up unattended?
    After taking an objective, crash side lane waves before regrouping. Lost side waves = lost gold that compounds.
WHEN BEHIND — The Deficit Playbook
  • ✕
    Stop fighting in fog of war
    All-ins when behind = feeding their bounties. You give them more gold, they get further ahead. Every blind fight you take when losing compounds the deficit.
  • ✕
    Don't let them have free Baron — zone with threat, not with a fight
    Stand at the edge. Force them to smite early. One good steal flip is worth more than five lost 5v5 Baron fights.
  • ✓
    Freeze your side lanes under your towers
    Deny them free farm, protect your carries from being caught, and farm yourself back into the game safely.
  • ✓
    Play for picks with deep vision, not straight fights
    Get deep wards. Wait. One pick creates a 5v4. 5v4 gets you an objective. Objective gets you back in the game.
  • ✓
    Remember: Comeback XP is linear and aggressive in 2026
    You close level gaps faster than old seasons. Don't panic. A stolen Baron + 650 global XP + minion buff can flip a game that looked unwinnable.
  • ✓
    Identify who on your team can still win the game and play around them
    Even when behind, someone on your team has the smallest deficit. Protect them, funnel resources, and let them carry.
END GAME — When You Have Baron / Elder / Inhibitors
  • 1
    After Baron: push the nearest inhibitor lane IMMEDIATELY with the minions
    Don't group in jungle. Don't look for fights. The buff is a siege tool, not a fight buff.
  • 2
    After an inhibitor: super minions apply constant pressure. Use that to force a fight on your terms.
    Super minions force the enemy to be in base or lose it. That creates free pressure everywhere else on the map.
  • 3
    Two inhibitors = Elder Dragon priority over everything
    Two inhibs + Elder Dragon buff = the game is over if you get it. Rotate immediately.
  • ✕
    Never recall to base unless you're near death
    Every second you spend in base at the end of the game with Baron buff ticking is gold thrown away. Heal at fountain only if you must.

Role-Specific Macro

General macro plus the specific decisions and math for each position on the map.

You know laning. Top lane macro problem is almost always the same: "I won lane. Now what?" The answer is not "keep farming top lane." The answer is a specific, phase-by-phase rotation plan.
Top Lane Macro Priority Order
  • 01
    At 14:00: crash top wave and rotate to Rift Herald or Dragon
    Sitting in top lane past 14 minutes when your team is contesting objectives means you're a liability, not an asset.
  • 02
    After every objective: TP back to top and continue split pressure
    This is the single biggest thing Challenger top laners do that Platinum players don't. Teleport creates a second self.
  • 03
    Ask: am I the split threat or the teamfighter?
    Split threat: stay in 1-3-1 all game, only group for Baron/Elder/definitive fights.
    Teamfighter: group at 15 min and anchor your team's front line. Don't split when you're the engage tool.
  • 04
    Teleport Usage — the math
    TP to tower → collect plates (~160g), crash wave, rotate. TP to a fight only if you arrive in time to add a decisive number.
    Bad TP to dying fight = you feed 2 people and waste a 4-minute CD.
  • 05
    Tower plates are now permanent (2026) — even on base towers
    Collect them constantly. A top laner who farms plates throughout the game accrues 800-1200g more than one who ignores them.
Split Push Math
Is Your Split Effective? You drawing 2 enemies + your 4 take Baron/objective = net win
