Wave Management Math

The most controllable macro lever. Master waves and tempo follows.

Waves are the game's clock. Every wave = ~177g and ~500 XP. Missing 3 waves early = half a level deficit. Wave state determines whether you have tempo — and tempo determines who wins every objective fight.
The Four Wave States
Freeze
3-5 more enemy minions near your tower. Enemy must walk into danger to farm. You are safe. They bleed gold and XP every second.
WHEN: Won lane, their jungler nearby, protecting a lead before an objective.
HOW: Kill only enough to maintain the ratio. Never over-kill. Cannon waves break freezes — kill cannon early.
Slow Push
Kill casters first. Let melee minions stack. Wave grows large automatically, creating a crashing window for roams or objectives.
WHEN: Before an objective spawn, before a roam, or to force a crash timing window.
HOW: Kill casters early each cycle. Last-hit only. Wave inflates over 30-60 seconds.
Crash
Full-clear everything into their tower. Tower kills their minions. Enemy loses CS if absent. You gain free tempo anywhere on the map.
WHEN: 30-40s before objective, before recalling, after kills, before roaming, to deny TP value.
HOW: Hard clear every minion. Push to tower. Leave as it crashes. Tempo window is yours.
Bounce
After a crash, enemy wave rebuilds and pushes toward you naturally. Safe setup for a freeze or farm window on return.
WHEN: Automatically happens after any crash. Use the window (recall, ward, roam).
HOW: Nothing — the wave handles itself. Return in time to collect the incoming wave.

XP Per Wave (early game) Full wave: ~500 XP
3 missed waves: ~1,500 XP lost
Level 4-6: 3 missed waves ≈ half a level behind
Crash Timing Formula Objective spawns at time T. Start crashing by: T − 40 seconds. Arrive: T − 10 to 15 seconds.
Still farming at T−20: drop the wave. Leave now. You are already late.
Freeze Ratio Formula Enemy minions near your tower must outnumber yours by 3 to 5.
Kill only what maintains that ratio. Never auto-attack if it tips the wave toward their tower.
Cannon waves naturally break freezes — kill cannon before it inflates the wave too much.
Roam Readiness — All 5 Must Be True
Roam Checklist
Readiness0 / 5
  • ✓
    Wave is crashing or already crashed into their tower
    If wave is pushing TO you → stay and collect. ~200g and 500 XP lost per missed wave.
  • ✓
    You have lane priority — enemy pushed in or recalled
    No priority = you arrive late and the fight is already decided. Wasted a wave for zero impact.
  • ✓
    There is a confirmed objective or kill opportunity to convert
    Roaming to "see what happens" is not a plan. Know your destination before leaving lane.
  • ✓
    You have enough HP to fight when you arrive
    60% HP roam = two kills fed instead of one. Recall first if needed — wave will still be there.
  • ✓
    You can physically walk there in time to matter
    Bot to top = ~20s walk. Calculate it. Fight ends before you arrive: TP or don't go.
The #1 Wave Error: Letting your wave crash randomly with no plan attached. Every crash must be deliberate — for a recall, roam, dive, or objective. If you can't answer "what am I doing with this crash?" you don't have a game plan.
