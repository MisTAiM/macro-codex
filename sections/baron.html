Baron is Now an XP Play

Global gold was cut nearly in half. Baron is now primarily about the 650 global XP spike — pushing your entire team to the next level simultaneously, unlocking item slots and ability rank-ups across all five members at once.

The minion buff remains the conversion tool. Use the buff to push and end — not to look for fights in fog.

The Baron Playbook — Four Phases
1
Threat — Create the Dilemma
Three in pit starting Baron. Two split-pushing side lanes hard. Enemy must choose: give up side towers OR come 5v3 at Baron. Either answer is wrong for them.
If they come 5v3: your two take free towers. If they don't: Baron is free.
2
Bait — Control the Tempo
Start Baron damage to force their hand. If they don't contest, finish it. If they do: do you have numbers? Is their position bad? Can your smite steal it?
Execute needs: numbers advantage + vision control + waves pushing in + smite ready.
3
Execute — Secure It Cleanly
Smite holder is the priority protect target. Everyone else peels for them. Smite when Baron reaches ~1,500-2,000 HP if contested. Never lose Baron to a steal when you had control.
Smite base damage: ~900 at level 18. Contested: smite at 1,000 HP to guarantee the secure.
4
Convert — Do Not Waste the Buff
After securing: push the nearest inhibitor lane with empowered minions right away. Do NOT group in fog. Buff is a siege tool — apply it to a tower now.
Every second of Baron buff not applied to a structure is tempo thrown directly in the trash.
