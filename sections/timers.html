Enemy Summoner Spell Tracking
Tracking enemy summoner spells is free information every game. When you see Flash, TP, or Ignite burned — note the game time. Add the cooldown. Fight before it comes back. Type it in chat so your whole team knows.
Flash 5:00 CD
Track Priority: S-Tier
The most impactful summoner in the game. Flash down on an enemy laner means you can all-in freely, execute a tower dive, or roam knowing they can't follow or escape. Flash down on an enemy jungler cuts their gank threat significantly — they can't close distance or escape counter-ganks.
Rule: Flash used at 8:30 → back at 13:30. Type "Flash [name] 8:30" in chat. Every fight between now and 13:30 is played with an enormous advantage.
Teleport → Tower target: 4:00 → Non-tower target: 5:00 → Cancelled mid-cast: 3:20 → Unleashed TP (top quest): 7:00
Track Priority: A-Tier — Especially Top vs Top
TP controls global macro. Enemy top TP down = they cannot respond to Dragon or Baron for 4-7 minutes without walking. You have a full global-presence window to roam, rotate, and apply cross-map pressure. When your own TP is down, you are committed to your side — plan around it.

Why CDs differ: Tower TP is shorter because landing there has less inherent map impact than a non-tower target. Cancellation is punished lightly at 3:20 — partial punishment for an inefficient action.
2026 note: Top laners who completed their Role Quest have Unleashed TP at 7:00 CD free on top of their two summoners. This means top lane always has a TP available — but it has a longer cooldown window to plan around.
Ignite / Exhaust / Ghost 3:30 CD
Track Priority: B-Tier — High Value in Lane Phase
Ignite: Determines who wins level 2-7 all-ins. If their support or laner burns it and misses, your kill pressure window is open for 3:30. Force the all-in immediately — do not wait.

Exhaust: Their carry or assassin's anti-burst tool is gone. Your burst champion can one-shot freely for 3:30. Coordinate dives and all-ins around this window.

Ghost: Their disengage or chase tool. Ghost down on a melee top = you can kite or chase freely. Ghost down on an ADC = they cannot run from your assassin.
Rule: Ignite burns and misses in lane → immediately trade. You have 3:30 of the most aggressive window in the laning phase. Do not sit back passively.
Smite — Enemy Jungler ~15s CD
Track Priority: S-Tier — Every Contested Objective
Enemy Smite is the most important piece of information in any Baron or Dragon contest. A jungler who just Smited a large camp has a ~15-second window where they cannot counter-Smite your objective. That is your steal-proof window.

You do not need to see Smite used directly. Watch the minimap: if their jungler just cleared Red Buff, Blue Buff, Gromp, Krugs, or any large camp, they fired Smite. You have 15 seconds.
At Baron specifically: If you see their jungler clear a camp at 19:40, start Baron at 19:50. They cannot counter-Smite until ~19:55. You get the first uncontested Smite window every time you apply this read.
The Tracking Chat Template — Make This a Habit "[Spell] [role/name] [time used]" → comes back at [time + CD] "Flash Garen 9:20" → back 14:20 → free dive window until then "TP Fiora 11:00 (tower)" → back 15:00 → Dragon at 12:00 is a safe 4v5 window "Smite Xin 18:45" → back ~18:58 → start Baron IMMEDIATELY if it has spawned "Ignite Thresh 6:30" → back 10:00 → all-in ADC freely for next 3:30 Typing this in chat costs you nothing and gives your entire team decision-making information they otherwise don't have.

2026 Role Quests — Completion Rewards by Role
Role Quests are the single biggest macro variable unique to 2026. Each role's quest completion triggers a power spike that changes your macro game plan. Know when YOU complete yours. Know when the ENEMY completes theirs. Force fights after your spike — before theirs.
