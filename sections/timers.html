2026 Role Quests — Completion Rewards by Role
Role Quests are the single biggest macro variable unique to 2026. Each role's quest completion triggers a power spike that changes your macro game plan. Know when YOU complete yours. Know when the ENEMY completes theirs. Force fights after your spike — before theirs.
RolePoints RequiredQuest RewardMacro Implication
Top Lane 1,200 pts Level cap → 20, +600 XP, +12.5% future XP, Unleashed TP (7-min CD) or TP shield (30% max HP, 30s) Farm longer than any other role. Level 19-20 vs level 18 = catastrophic stat lead. TP shield enables aggressive TPs.
Jungle Objective-based +4% MS in jungle/river (8% out of combat), +10g and +10 XP per large monster kill Every large camp is more valuable post-quest. Objective pressure increases. Path toward large camps more aggressively.
Mid Lane 1,350 pts T2 boots upgrade to T3 automatically, empowered 4-second recall every 5 minutes Recall timing changes — 4s recall is a significant advantage for reset cycles. T3 boots = permanent movement advantage.
Bot Lane (ADC) 1,350 pts 7th item slot, long-term gold scaling based on performance After quest: ADC has a 7th item slot giving permanent stat advantage. Every subsequent minute the ADC farms, the scaling widens.
Support Item-based Support item upgrade, empowered recall every 5 minutes Empowered recall lets support reset to full before critical objective windows. Time recalls around Dragon/Baron setup.
