2026 Kill Gold System — Doubled in Patch 26.1
Both rates doubled in 26.1. Devaluation 0.2 → 0.4. Bounty accrual 0.1 → 0.2. Dying punishes you twice as hard. Your fed carry is twice the target they were last season.
Kill Gold Devaluation (2026) Per 1g you give on death: −0.4g from your base kill value

You die giving 300g → next kill worth 300 − (300 × 0.4) = 120g less
Old system (2025): only 60g less

Dying is now twice as expensive. Stop feeding.
Bounty Accrual (Also Doubled) Per 1g earned: bounty grows by 0.2g (was 0.1)

Carry at 10,000g earned total:
Old bounty: ~1,000g | New bounty: ~2,000g

Your fed carry is twice the target. Play around them or split them where it is safe.
Baron XP Math — Why Baron is Now a Level Play OLD: 25g kill + 300g global = 325g net. Small local XP only.
NEW: 100g kill + 150g global = 250g net. +650 XP GLOBAL to all 5 teammates.

650 XP at level 12 = roughly 40-60% of the next level for your whole team simultaneously.
Taking Baron at level 13 can spike everyone toward 14 at the same time.
Comeback XP applies to Baron too. Losing team stealing Baron = massive level spike.
Optimal Recall Formula — All Should Be True ✓ Wave crashing or crashed (zero CS loss on recall)
✓ 1,400-1,600g+ threshold (full component or complete item on buy)
✓ No objective spawning in the next 90 seconds
✓ Lane opponent at similar HP or also recalling

Good recall = item advantage + safe return into bounced wave
Bad recall = 300-440g in lost waves + pushed wave you cannot freeze on return
