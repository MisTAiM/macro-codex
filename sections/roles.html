Role-Specific Macro

General macro applied to each position's unique movement patterns.

You know laning. Top macro problem is almost always: "I won lane. Now what?" The answer is not "keep farming top lane." Here is the phase-by-phase rotation plan every Challenger top laner follows.
Top Lane Macro Order
Top Lane0 / 6
  • 01
    At 14:00: crash top wave and rotate to Herald or Dragon
    Sitting in top lane past 14 min while team contests objectives = liability, not a player.
  • 02
    After every objective: TP back to top and continue split pressure
    The single biggest thing Challenger top laners do that Gold players miss. TP creates a second self.
  • 03
    Decide: am I the split threat or the teamfighter?
    Split: 1-3-1 all game, only group for Baron/Elder. Teamfight: group at 15 min and anchor frontline.
  • 04
    TP math: use it for real value, not ego saves
    TP to tower = ~160g plates + crash + rotate. TP to dying fight = two people fed and 4-min CD wasted.
  • 05
    Collect tower plates constantly — permanent in 2026
    Plates are permanent now including base towers. Farming them all game = 800-1200g advantage.
  • 06
    Check: how many enemies are responding to my split?
    Two or more responding: stay and draw them. Zero responding: stop splitting. You are useless. Group.
Split Push MathYou drawing 2 enemies + 4 teammates take Baron = net win
You drawing 1 = situational | You drawing 0 = useless. Group immediately.

Test: after 30s in side lane, how many pinged you? 2+ = keep splitting. 0-1 = group now.
You know laning. ADC macro problem: "I don't know where to be, when to group, or what to hit in teamfights." Three specific answers below.
ADC Macro Order
ADC Lane0 / 6
  • 01
    At 14:00: crash bot wave and rotate to Dragon
    Bot lane is Dragon side. You set up nearly every Dragon fight. This is your macro role after laning ends.
  • 02
    After Dragon: never sit in bot lane farming alone
    Group at mid or apply top pressure. Farming bot solo when the map is active = you are irrelevant.
  • 03
    Siege play: use range advantage to chip towers, not to duel
    Your job in sieges is hitting the tower while your team zones. Hitting their frontline = you die.
  • 04
    Teamfights: max range, hit the nearest valid target
    Nearest target is the correct target always. Repositioning across the fight to reach their carry = you die.
  • 05
    When you are the win condition: survive first, everything else second
    Most gold on team = your job is dealing damage without dying. You are the weapon, not the trigger.
  • 06
    Kite every fight — auto then move, auto then move
    Every ADC who dies in teamfights stopped moving. Auto-attack then move back. Never stand still.
Jungle macro is a loop: Clear → Gank or Objective → Clear → Gank or Objective. Every camp funds the next tempo play. Aimless clearing loses the game one camp at a time.
Jungle Macro Loop
Jungle0 / 6
  • 01
    Void Grubs at 6:00 — route your first clear to arrive there
    Six Grubs = maximum Voidmite tower push pressure. Missing them because of your path = major macro error.
  • 02
    Track enemy jungler position at all times
    Enemy seen top side = Dragon and bot are free. Ping bot immediately. Enemy seen bot = top is free.
  • 03
    Set up objectives 60 seconds early — Smite must be ready
    Smite on cooldown when Baron spawns = you auto-lose all contested Baron fights. Time your last camp around it.
  • 04
    Baron vision established by 18:30 — not at 19:45
    Five minutes earlier than last season. Routing to Baron at 19:30 means you are already late.
  • 05
    Path toward the next objective — not the easiest camp
    Easy camp is a trap. Clear camps that position you correctly — not camps that are simply convenient.
  • 06
    After every gank: what objective can I take with this advantage?
    Gank → kill → take the nearby objective immediately. Never gank and go back to farming without converting.
Support macro is vision plus enablement. You are the map's eyes and your carry's safety net. Your vision score IS your macro performance metric every single game.
Support Macro Order
Support0 / 6
  • 01
    Vision score 2.0+ per minute — this is your primary job
    Vision score under 1.0/min means you are not doing your job regardless of assists or healing done.
  • 02
    At 14:00: roam to Dragon setup with your jungler
    Your ADC loses one wave. Dragon is worth multiple waves. This trade is always correct — make it every game.
  • 03
    After any fight win: deep ward their jungle immediately
    Dead enemies = free warding window. This is your single most valuable action during the death timer window.
  • 04
    Keep your carry alive through peel — not by engaging first
    Dying "for your carry" by standing between them = no peel + they die anyway. Stand behind. Peel what reaches them.
  • 05
    Buy one Control Ward every single recall without exception
    75 gold. Highest gold efficiency in the game. Not buying one = playing without available information.
  • 06
    Baron river wards placed by 18:30 on every game
    Baron spawns at 20:00. You need complete river vision 90 seconds before. Planting at 19:45 = already lost.
Mid is the map's pivot point. Fastest access to both sides means your roam timing and wave management creates cascading advantages everywhere — but only when deliberate.
Mid Lane Macro Order
Mid Lane0 / 5
  • 01
    Slow push mid then crash then roam with priority
    Core mid-lane macro pattern. Repeat every 2-3 minutes throughout the laning phase without deviation.
  • 02
    Roam window: levels 6-14 around your ultimate cooldown
    After 14 min, staying in lane has diminishing returns unless you have TP. Roam aggressively around ult timing.
  • 03
    Hold mid priority so your team can set up objectives safely
    Mid priority = your jungle and support can set up Dragon without fear of enemy mid roaming to collapse.
  • 04
    Enemy mid roamed? Push their mid tower — do not follow
    Enemy mid left for bot: push mid tower. If you follow, you both lose your lanes for no gain.
  • 05
    Post-14: become the team objective anchor and shotcaller
    Mid is central geographically. Use that position to call rotations and set up every upcoming objective play.
