Role-Specific Macro

General macro applied to each position's unique movement patterns.

The "rotate at 14" rule does NOT apply to top lane. Top lane in 2026 has completely different macro rules than every other role. It is an island role with TP as its primary global tool and a level 20 cap that rewards staying in lane longer than anyone else. Read this section before the others.
The 2026 Top Lane Fundamentals — What Changed Everything
Top Lane Role Quest Rewards (2026 — 1,200 Points) Level cap raised to 20 (everyone else caps at 18) +600 XP immediately on quest completion All future XP gains increased by 12.5% permanently If you took Teleport: arrival now grants a 30% max health shield for 30 seconds (offensive TP tool) If you did NOT take Teleport: Unleashed Teleport granted free at 7-minute cooldown Anti-laneswap: minion gold/XP reduced 25% outside top lane until level 3 WHAT THIS MEANS MACRO-WISE: Top is the ONLY role that can realistically farm to level 20. At level 19-20 while the enemy team is level 18, your champion is playing a fundamentally different game than everyone else on the map. Your job is to EARN that level advantage — then weaponize it.
The Island Phase Math (0 to ~18 minutes) Farming top lane at 8 CS/min = ~168g/min in plates, CS, and tower gold Dragon is worth 150g global (×5 players = 750g team value) But: 750g / 168g/min = you need to "lose" only 4.5 minutes of top lane farming for Dragon to break even HOWEVER: If you die rotating without TP — you give a bounty (~300g) AND lose your death timer (~40s) AND lose the wave that crashed while you were dead AND give them free CS on your side Total cost of a failed rotation: 300g bounty + 2-3 waves (350-530g) = 650-830g swing CONCLUSION: Only rotate when TP is available OR you can walk there in time AND survive the fight. Farming top and TPing is ALWAYS better than walking mid-game if your TP is up.
TP Decision Tree — The Core of Top Lane Macro
Primary Question — Before Every TP
Does this TP create a decisive numbers advantage that we can immediately convert to an objective or kill?
Yes → Use TP
Examples: team is fighting Dragon 4v5, team is fighting Baron 4v5, a winning teamfight is happening bot and you can take the tower + Dragon after, a dive is set up and your arrival flips it. Your TP shield makes offensive TPs stronger in 2026.
No → Farm Top
If the fight is already over (won or lost), if it's just a skirmish with no objective, if you're walking into a losing fight — stay top. Crash your wave, collect the plates, and set up the NEXT TP play.
TP Cooldown States — Know Which Phase You Are In TP up + wave crashing = MAXIMUM power state. You can farm, crash, and TP to any objective. TP on cooldown (7 min base, or 7 min Unleashed) = you are committed to your side of the map. During TP cooldown: Do NOT walk to objectives. Crash waves, collect plates, ward river. When TP comes back: immediately think "what is the next objective I can TP for?" 2026 NOTE: If you took TP as your spell and completed your Role Quest, your TP grants a 30% max health shield on arrival. This means you can TP INTO fights aggressively, not just defensively. Treat the shield as ~500-800 effective HP depending on your max health at the time.
When Top Lane Stays Until 20 Minutes
This is the decision that most Gold players get wrong. Farming top until 20 minutes is NOT throwing — it is correct macro in specific conditions. Here is when it is right, when it is wrong, and the math behind it.
Farm Top Until ~18-20 min WHEN:
  • ✓ You are winning your 1v1 and denying enemy CS — your gold lead stays ahead even staying top
  • ✓ Your TP is available — you can reach any objective in time via TP
  • ✓ Your champion scales with gold — (Nasus, Fiora, Camille, Garen after level advantage)
  • ✓ Enemy top is also top side — nullifies the 4v4 vs. 4v5 disadvantage
  • ✓ Void Grubs were secured by your team — Voidmites attack towers, your split push has extra power
  • ✓ You are getting plates — every plate you collect is irreversible gold your team doesn't have to share
Do NOT Farm Top Past 18 min WHEN:
  • ✕ Your TP is on cooldown — you have zero global presence. Baron at 20:00 is uncontestable by you.
  • ✕ You are losing your 1v1 — you are not generating gold, just bleeding XP and being zoned
  • ✕ Baron spawns in <90 seconds and your team is 4 — a 4v5 Baron fight loses you the entire game state
  • ✕ Zero enemies responding to your split — you are not applying pressure. Group immediately.
  • ✕ Your team comp needs you in fights to win — (engage tank, hard CC initiator) — your teamfight is the win condition
The 20-Minute Window — Baron Timing Changes Top Lane Macro Baron spawns at 20:00. This is 5 minutes earlier than last season. Top lane used to have until 25:00 before Baron was relevant. Now it is 20:00. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR TOP: Your latest possible TP to Baron is: 19:00-19:30 (gives you time to arrive and set up) If your TP cooldown will not be up by 19:30: you must have WALKED to Baron side by 19:00 If you cannot do either: your team is fighting a 4v5 Baron fight at 20:00. TIMING YOUR TP COOLDOWN AROUND BARON: If TP goes on cooldown at 13:00: it comes back at 20:00 (7 min CD). Exact Baron spawn. TP ready. If TP goes on cooldown at 14:00: comes back at 21:00. You MISS the first Baron contest. If TP goes on cooldown at 15:00: comes back at 22:00. You miss first Baron. Plan your TP usage around this. Burning TP at 14:00 means missing Baron at 20:00.
Void Grubs and Top Lane — What Most Players Miss
Grubs Give Top Lane Split Pushers a Tower-Shredding Multiplier Void Grubs (spawn 6:00, Wave 2 @ 10:00): each gives your team Voidmite stacks Voidmites are creatures that spawn and attack towers when you attack them 3+ Grubs = significant tower damage acceleration on every structure hit 6 Grubs = maximum Voidmite output — towers fall dramatically faster FOR TOP LANE SPECIFICALLY: If your team secured 4-6 Grubs, your split push is exponentially stronger. Grub stacks effectively reduce the time to take a tower from ~30s to significantly less. A top laner with 6-stack Grub pressure + Crystalline Overgrowth can collapse an outer tower fast. This is why Grubs are a higher priority for split-push comps than dive/engage comps.
Top Lane Role Quest Checklist
Top Lane Macro Decisions
Top Lane0 / 8
  • 01
    Is my TP up or on cooldown? Plan the next 7 minutes around this answer.
    TP up = global presence, farm freely and TP to objectives. TP down = commit to your side. Do not walk to objectives.
  • 02
    Am I winning my 1v1? If yes, farm. If no, freeze and survive.
    Winning = keep farming and generating lead. Losing = freeze under tower, deny them free CS, wait for jungler, do not fight.
  • 03
    Collect every plate. They are local gold that does not split with your team.
    Plates are permanent in 2026 — even on base towers. A top laner farming plates all game accumulates 600-1,200g more than one who ignores them.
  • 04
    Am I the split pusher or the teamfighter? Commit to one answer before 15:00.
    Split: 1-3-1 all game, only TP for Baron/Elder/crucial fight. Teamfight: walk to objectives at 15-17 min and anchor frontline. Trying to do both does neither.
  • 05
    TP cooldown math: will my TP be up for Baron at 20:00?
    TP burned at 13:00 = back by 20:00 exactly. Burned at 14:00 = you miss first Baron. Know this before you TP for anything pre-20.
  • 06
    Is my Role Quest complete? If yes, my level cap is 20 — stay in lane longer to leverage this.
    Level 19-20 vs. level 18 enemies is a catastrophic stat advantage. The quest rewards staying in lane. Use it — do not abandon the level lead by grouping too early.
  • 07
    How many enemies are responding to my split? 2+ = stay. 0 = group.
    The split push only works if it draws attention. If nobody is coming, you are farming irrelevant top and your 4 teammates are fighting 4v5.
  • 08
    After a team objective: TP back to top and continue applying pressure.
    This is the tempo loop. TP to Dragon/Baron fight → win it → TP back to top → crash wave → collect plates → repeat. Each cycle widens your lead.
Split Push Math — The Real Numbers You drawing 2 enemies: your team is 4v3 everywhere else on the map. Baron/Dragon 4v3 = decisive. Tower sieges 4v3 = free. You drawing 1 enemy: team has 4v4 fights everywhere. Neutral at best. You drawing 0 enemies: you are a ghost. Group immediately. You have zero macro impact. PLATE MATH: 5 plates per outer tower × 160g (to you, not split) = 800g if you take all 5 Plus the outer tower itself: 150-300g split (~50-100g to you) + 50g global Total top lane outer tower value to the top laner: ~850-1,000g That is more than a completed component item earned entirely from your own play. No other role can generate this kind of unshared gold from one objective.
You know laning. ADC macro problem: "I don't know where to be, when to group, or what to hit in teamfights." Three specific answers below.
ADC Macro Order
ADC Lane0 / 6
  • 01
    At 14:00: crash bot wave and rotate to Dragon
    Bot lane is Dragon side. You set up nearly every Dragon fight. This is your macro role after laning ends.
  • 02
    After Dragon: never sit in bot lane farming alone
    Group at mid or apply top pressure. Farming bot solo when the map is active = you are irrelevant.
  • 03
    Siege play: use range advantage to chip towers, not to duel
    Your job in sieges is hitting the tower while your team zones. Hitting their frontline = you die.
  • 04
    Teamfights: max range, hit the nearest valid target
    Nearest target is the correct target always. Repositioning across the fight to reach their carry = you die.
  • 05
    When you are the win condition: survive first, everything else second
    Most gold on team = your job is dealing damage without dying. You are the weapon, not the trigger.
  • 06
    Kite every fight — auto then move, auto then move
    Every ADC who dies in teamfights stopped moving. Auto-attack then move back. Never stand still.
Jungle macro is a loop: Clear → Gank or Objective → Clear → Gank or Objective. Every camp funds the next tempo play. Aimless clearing loses the game one camp at a time.
Jungle Macro Loop
Jungle0 / 6
  • 01
    Void Grubs at 6:00 — route your first clear to arrive there
    Six Grubs = maximum Voidmite tower push pressure. Missing them because of your path = major macro error.
  • 02
    Track enemy jungler position at all times
    Enemy seen top side = Dragon and bot are free. Ping bot immediately. Enemy seen bot = top is free.
  • 03
    Set up objectives 60 seconds early — Smite must be ready
    Smite on cooldown when Baron spawns = you auto-lose all contested Baron fights. Time your last camp around it.
  • 04
    Baron vision established by 18:30 — not at 19:45
    Five minutes earlier than last season. Routing to Baron at 19:30 means you are already late.
  • 05
    Path toward the next objective — not the easiest camp
    Easy camp is a trap. Clear camps that position you correctly — not camps that are simply convenient.
  • 06
    After every gank: what objective can I take with this advantage?
    Gank → kill → take the nearby objective immediately. Never gank and go back to farming without converting.
Support macro is vision plus enablement. You are the map's eyes and your carry's safety net. Your vision score IS your macro performance metric every single game.
Support Macro Order
Support0 / 6
  • 01
    Vision score 2.0+ per minute — this is your primary job
    Vision score under 1.0/min means you are not doing your job regardless of assists or healing done.
  • 02
    At 14:00: roam to Dragon setup with your jungler
    Your ADC loses one wave. Dragon is worth multiple waves. This trade is always correct — make it every game.
  • 03
    After any fight win: deep ward their jungle immediately
    Dead enemies = free warding window. This is your single most valuable action during the death timer window.
  • 04
    Keep your carry alive through peel — not by engaging first
    Dying "for your carry" by standing between them = no peel + they die anyway. Stand behind. Peel what reaches them.
  • 05
    Buy one Control Ward every single recall without exception
    75 gold. Highest gold efficiency in the game. Not buying one = playing without available information.
  • 06
    Baron river wards placed by 18:30 on every game
    Baron spawns at 20:00. You need complete river vision 90 seconds before. Planting at 19:45 = already lost.
Mid is the map's pivot point. Fastest access to both sides means your roam timing and wave management creates cascading advantages everywhere — but only when deliberate.
Mid Lane Macro Order
Mid Lane0 / 5
  • 01
    Slow push mid then crash then roam with priority
    Core mid-lane macro pattern. Repeat every 2-3 minutes throughout the laning phase without deviation.
  • 02
    Roam window: levels 6-14 around your ultimate cooldown
    After 14 min, staying in lane has diminishing returns unless you have TP. Roam aggressively around ult timing.
  • 03
    Hold mid priority so your team can set up objectives safely
    Mid priority = your jungle and support can set up Dragon without fear of enemy mid roaming to collapse.
  • 04
    Enemy mid roamed? Push their mid tower — do not follow
    Enemy mid left for bot: push mid tower. If you follow, you both lose your lanes for no gain.
  • 05
    Post-14: become the team objective anchor and shotcaller
    Mid is central geographically. Use that position to call rotations and set up every upcoming objective play.
