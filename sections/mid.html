Mid-Game Systems

When laning ends, most players follow teammates aimlessly. Don't.

Most Important Macro Skill: Knowing what to do in the 5 seconds after a fight ends. That window determines whether wins become leads or evaporate. Run this priority system every single time.
Post-Fight Priority — Click Each Step as You Execute It
1
Can we end the game right now?
If yes: walk to Nexus. Ignore everything else. No "playing safe." The game ending beats every other objective.
2
Is Baron spawning or available in the next 90 seconds?
If yes: rotate immediately. Vision first. Smite ready. Baron at 20:00 — win a fight at 19:30 and that Baron is yours.
3
Is Dragon spawning or available in the next 90 seconds?
If yes: rotate to Dragon. Every stack compounds permanently. Trading one tower for an early Drake stack is usually correct.
4
Is there a tower in our direct path?
If yes: push it down. Always push in the direction of the fight. Never regroup across the whole map empty-handed.
5
Should we reset for a major item spike?
Only if: no objectives in 90s AND gold within 300g of a major spike AND waves in safe state. Otherwise: wasting tempo.
6
Push side lanes and set up the next play cycle.
Crash a side wave. Ward the next objective area. Force the enemy to make a decision. Repeat from Step 1.
60-30-10 Objective Setup Rule
T−60 secVision
Ward river entrances, pit flanks, approach paths. Sweep enemy wards from your side. Whole team on correct side of the map — not farming, not in base, moving with intent.
T−30 secStack
Stop farming completely. Everyone at the objective. Check minimap for all 5 enemy positions. If 3+ enemies are unaccounted for: do NOT start — wait or disengage.
T−10 secExecute
Smite ready. Frontline tanking. Formation set. Engage on THEM if they approach. You arrived first — you dictate the angle, the terms, and the tempo.
Map Split Patterns
1-3-1 Split
One splits top, one splits bot, three control mid and vision. Side laners must draw attention or the pattern fails. Group only for Baron, Elder, or a decisive fight.
Use when: Two champions who can 1v1 or 1v2 in side lanes and disengage safely.
Drawing 2 enemies + 4 take Baron = net win. Drawing 0 = group right now.
4-1 Death Push
One splits drawing a response, four group mid to siege or wait for picks. The solo player must create a 2v1 or threaten a tower or the pattern accomplishes nothing.
Use when: Fed tank or bruiser who can split endlessly and TP back to big fights.
1 draws 2 = 4v3 mid advantage. 1 draws 0 = group immediately.
5v5 Grouping
Everyone pushes mid together to force a fight, siege, or objective. Works when you're decisively ahead or your comp needs all five to activate its win condition.
Use when: AoE teamfight comp, or so far ahead individual plays carry no risk.
Every second grouped without taking a tower = wasted GPM across all five players.
Pick and Flank
Deep vision, bait with one player, collapse with four on an isolated target. Requires excellent vision control and reliable CC to lock the target before their team responds.
Use when: Assassins, engage supports, any comp whose win condition is "kill one, take everything."
1 pick = 5v4 for 25-40s = guaranteed objective if you convert immediately.
Role Quests 2026: Every role has a lane-phase quest with a real power spike reward. Know when yours completes. Know when the enemy's does. Force fights after your quest — before theirs.
