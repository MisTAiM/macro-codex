Vision Items — Know What Each Does
Stealth Ward
Standard trinket. 1 charge, ~3 min duration. Always placed before pushing into unwarded terrain.
Control Ward
75g. Reveals and disables enemy wards. Permanent until killed. Buy on every single recall — no exceptions.
Oracle Lens
Sweeper. Detects and clears enemy wards in a radius. Use before starting Dragon or Baron. Sweep pit first.
Vigilant Wardstone
Upgrade — increases ward slots and Control Ward cap. Support / jungler endgame item for maximum vision output.
Ward vs Sweep Decision Trees
Decision Point
Is an objective spawning in the next 90 seconds?
Yes →
Sweep first, then plant. Clear enemy wards in pit and river BEFORE placing yours. Their ward watching you plant = they see your position and counter-engage with perfect timing. Order: sweep pit → sweep river → plant deep offensive wards.
No →
Defensive plant only. River bushes, tribrush on your side, approach paths. Save Oracle charges for pre-objective timing — don't waste charges on lanes with zero strategic value.
Decision Point
Did your team just win a fight with 20-40 seconds of dead enemies?
Yes →
Deep ward immediately. Place in their jungle — their blue side, camp paths, lane approaches. This is your free information window. Support: this is your single highest-value action right now. Do not recall yet.
No →
Stay in safe positions only. Never deep ward when enemies are alive and unaccounted for. You lose both the ward and your death timer — the exact opposite of what vision control should accomplish.
