Pre-Game — During Pick Phase
Pre-Game
Pre-Game Progress0 / 5
  • 01
    What is OUR win condition?
    Scale / Pick / Split / Engage / Siege. One answer. Commit. If you cannot name it, you do not have one.
  • 02
    What is THEIR win condition?
    Scale = force early fights. Engage = deny their vision. Pick = group up and never roam solo.
  • 03
    When does our comp peak vs theirs?
    Peak before them = fight on your timeline. Peak after = stall to your window, deny their early fights.
  • 04
    What is my matchup expectation?
    Favored = aggressive for kills and priority. Unfavored = safe farm and scale, deny their pressure.
  • 05
    Do I know my Role Quest objective?
    Season 2026 — every role has a lane-phase quest with a real power spike. Know what yours requires.
Laning Loop — Every 2 Minutes
2-Minute Scan
Lane Loop0 / 7

    What is my wave state and is it where I want it?
    Pushed = roam priority. Frozen = denying them. Crashing = tempo window. Bouncing = collect safely.

    Where is the enemy jungler right now?
    Unseen for 90s and river dark = respect the gank. Pull back. Do not be greedy for one more CS.
  • →
    Is an objective spawning in the next 2 minutes?
    If yes: crash wave now. Arriving with priority 30s before spawn = you control the objective fight.
  • →
    What are the enemy summoner spell cooldowns?
    No Flash (5 min) = you can all-in, dive, or roam safely. Track from the moment it gets burned.
  • →
    Should I recall — healthy and at a gold threshold?
    Below 40% HP with wave crashed = recall. Staying low for 2 more CS is a false economy every time.
  • →
    Am I progressing my Role Quest objective?
    2026 quest spike is real. Play toward completing it — and track when the enemy completes theirs.
  • →
    What am I doing with my next wave crash?
    Every crash needs a plan: recall / roam / objective / vision / dive setup. "Nothing" is not acceptable.
Post-Fight — Immediate Actions After Winning
Post-Fight Conversion
Conversion0 / 6
  • 1
    Count enemy death timers — how long is the window?
    Level 13 = ~40s. Level 15 = ~47s. Start counting from the kill. That is your conversion window.
  • 2
    Can we end the game right now?
    If yes: go. Skip everything else. Nothing matters more than the game ending immediately.
  • 3
    Highest priority objective in reach?
    Order: End game → Baron → Dragon → Tower. Take the highest thing you can reach in time.
  • 4
    Is there a tower in our direct path?
    Always push in the direction of the fight. Never regroup across the whole map empty-handed.
  • 5
    Within 300 gold of a major item spike?
    If objectives clear and you are 300g from a spike: recall, buy, return before the next window.
  • 6
    Side waves building up unattended?
    Crash side waves before regrouping. Lost side waves compound against you silently every minute.
When Behind — Deficit Survival
Deficit Playbook
Deficit Controls0 / 6
  • ✕
    Stop fighting in fog of war
    All-ins when behind = feeding bounties. Every blind fight compounds the deficit through doubled devaluation.
  • ✕
    Do not 5v5 fight for Baron — zone with threat only
    Stand at pit entrance. Force their early smite. One steal flip beats five lost Baron fights.
  • ✓
    Freeze lanes under your towers and farm back in
    Deny them free farm while safely scaling. Freezing is strategic denial — not passive play.
  • ✓
    Look for picks with deep vision — not straight fights
    Deep ward → wait for isolated target → collapse → 5v4 objective. That is your path back in.
  • ✓
    Remember comeback XP is linear and aggressive in 2026
    You close level gaps faster now. A stolen Baron + 650 XP + buff can flip unwinnable games.
  • ✓
    Identify who can still win the game and protect them
    Even when behind, someone has the smallest deficit. Fund them, protect them, stop trying to solo-carry.
End Game — Baron, Elder, Inhibitor Conversion
Game Closer
Closing0 / 5
  • 1
    After Baron: push nearest inhibitor lane with empowered minions now
    Do not group in jungle. Do not look for fights. Buff is a siege tool — apply it to a tower now.
  • 2
    After inhibitor: use super minion pressure to force a favorable fight
    Super minions force them to defend. Pick the fight angle you want — not the one they choose.
  • 3
    Two inhibitors = Elder Dragon is absolute top priority
    Two inhibs + Elder buff = the game is over. Rotate to Elder immediately and take it.
  • ✕
    Do NOT recall with Baron buff still ticking
    Every second in base with buff active is tempo thrown away. Fountain only if no other option.
  • ✕
    Do NOT look for fights in fog just because you have buff
    Baron buff wins sieges not fights. Push the tower. Do not brawl in their jungle for no reason.
